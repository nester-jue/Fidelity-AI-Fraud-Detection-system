# Knowledge Package — Compliance Officer

- Department: compliance_officer
- Package: compliance_officer
- Scope: department
- Status: verified
- Owner-role: compliance_officer

## Overview

Compliance monitors regulatory adherence and internal policy. The assistant
explains compliance status and requirements — it does not change anything.

## Regulatory Compliance

- Applicable insurance regulations (Zimbabwe insurance regulations, the
  Insurance Act and any statutory code of practice).
- Insurance policy requirements — customer identification, coverage
  limits, exclusions, notices.
- Customer identification requirements — KYC procedures (Identity,
  residence, occupation verification).
- Anti-money laundering controls — suspicious transaction reporting,
  enhanced due diligence.
- Data protection and confidentiality — access limits, audit, encryption.
- Record retention requirements — how long claims, assessments and evidence
  must be kept.
- Internal company policies — approved procedure documents.

## Compliance Monitoring

- Claims documentation requirements — completeness at assessment time.
- Customer verification — checks against KYC files before payments.
- Policy compliance checks — versions, dates, endorsements.
- Internal control procedures — segments of work, approvals, audit trails.
- Investigation compliance — evidence rules, fairness, procedures.
- Regulatory reporting — submission deadlines and formats.
- Compliance exceptions — documented gaps and remediation plans.
- Audit findings — from internal and external audits, trend review.
- Corrective actions — follow-up on outstanding exceptions.

## Governance

- Employee responsibilities — duties of all staff in compliance process.
- Approval procedures — who signs off, required evidence, escalation.
- Risk management policies — enterprise risk framework.
- Internal audit requirements — audit schedule, sample methods.
- Reporting procedures — who receives compliance reports and when.
- Escalation protocols — breach response: containment, notification, review.

## FAQs

- Which documents are required for customer verification?
- What happens when a claim fails a compliance check?
- Which cases have outstanding compliance requirements?
- What information should be included in an audit report?
- How should suspicious activity be escalated?

## Escalation

Escalate regulatory, legal or major compliance issues to the CRO and legal
counsel. All suspected breaches must be logged as compliance exceptions.

## Restricted Information

Compliance file data and audit logs are restricted to Compliance and
authorised Executive Management. PII belongs to the individuals involved.
