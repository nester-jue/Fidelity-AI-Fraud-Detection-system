# Knowledge Package — Claims Manager

- Department: claims_manager
- Package: claims_manager
- Scope: department
- Status: verified
- Owner-role: claims_manager

## Overview

Claims Managers own the Claims department portfolio: they review,
approve/reject claims, assign officers, manage outstanding work and report
performance.

## Claims Department Operations

- All claims in the department portfolio (assigned to your officers or
  unassigned awaiting assignment).
- Assessment and approval procedures: officer recommends, manager reviews,
  decision logged with approval reason.
- Claims allocation and workload management: balance assignments across
  officers; review claims over average outstanding time.
- Claims officer responsibilities: their quality, documentation completeness
  and approval turnaround.
- Claims escalation procedures: flagged/high-risk claims → SIU/Fraud
  Investigation Unit; overdue claims → officer follow-up.
- Claims turnaround times: defined per department SLA; monitor each officer.
- Outstanding claims management: daily review of oldest outstanding items.
- Claims quality assurance: sampling for documentation and procedure
  compliance; irregularity reports to Compliance.
- High-value claims review: involvement in High/Critical risk claims above
  a defined threshold; document justification for approval or rejection.
- Claims rejection and reconsideration procedures: rejected claims may be
  reconsidered by the officer, then re-reviewed by the manager.

## Departmental Performance Indicators

- Claims processed vs. received (daily and monthly).
- Approval/rejection ratios.
- Average processing time per status stage.
- Outstanding claims count and age distribution.
- Documentation completeness rate at assessment time.
- Fraud alert volume raised by officers.

## Escalation

Escalate fraud risk to the Fraud Investigation Unit (SIU) immediately. For
complaints or disputes above your authority, escalate to Executive
Management.

## Restricted Information

Only Claims-department data is visible to you: other departments' fraud
details, investigations you do not own, and system-admin information are out
of scope.
