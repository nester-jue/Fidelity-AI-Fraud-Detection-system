## Overview

Fidelity Life Assurance Company is a life assurance and financial services
organisation. The assistant uses this shared knowledge alongside
department-specific packages and live system records.

**Rule for answers:** information here is company policy and marketing
context. Live database values (claim amounts, statuses, scores, counts) are
only from verified system records — never invent statistics.

## Products

- **Life Assurance** — permanent life cover protecting loved ones.
- **Group Life Assurance** — bulk life cover for employers and associations.
- **Funeral Assurance** — burial cover for the policyholder.
- **Endowment Policies** — policies where premiums and value are aligned.
- **Educational Policies** — cover for future educational needs.
- **Mortgage and Loan Protection** — coverage ensuring loan repayments during critical periods.
- **Annuity Products** — income streams in later life stages.
- **Medical Aid Products** — supplementary health coverage, where applicable.

## Financial Services

- **Personal Loans** — individual unsecured loans.
- **Salary-based Lending** — loans linked to employment income.
- **Agricultural Financing and Microfinance** — rural and small-business funding.

## Actuarial Services

Risk analysis, actuarial valuations, pension consulting, financial

## Policies

Approved company policies and procedures are published to the internal
knowledge base. Publicly available company information does not replace
internal policies: the assistant answers from verified internal sources only.

## Customer Service Standards

- Explain requirements clearly before asking customers to act.
- Distinguish missing documentation from system issues.
- Record all complaints with reference numbers.
- Never commit to timelines outside the department SLA.

## Fraud Prevention

- Red-flag indicators: inflated amounts, false death certificates, forged
  medical reports, duplicate documents, beneficiary changes, rushed claims.
- Prevention: verify against documents, OCR extraction confidence, and
  historical claim patterns.
- Escalation: suspicious activity goes to Fraud Investigation Unit.
