# Knowledge Package — Claims Officer

- Department: claims_officer
- Package: claims_officer
- Scope: department
- Status: verified
- Owner-role: claims_officer

## Overview

Claims Officers process submitted claims from the Claims department. The
assistant answers operational questions only — it never approves, rejects
or alters claims on its own.

## Products I Understand

Individual life assurance, group life assurance, funeral assurance,
endowment policies, educational policies, mortgage and loan protection,
annuity products, and medical aid products where applicable.

## Claims Processing

- **What constitutes an insurance claim** — a formal request for payment
  covered under a valid policy (death, funeral, medical, disability or
  maturity events per the policy type).
- **How a customer submits a claim** — through the Claims workspace, or by
  uploading supporting documents via Document Upload.
- **Claim registration procedures** — new claim record created, status set
  to `submitted`; no payment without complete documentation.
- **Claim eligibility requirements** — policy must be active at incident
  date, the event is covered, and the incident is not excluded.
- **Required supporting documents** — see Documentation below.
- **Policy verification** — check policy number, status, inception date and
  coverage limits before processing.
- **Claim assessment procedures** — officer review against documentation,
  AI fraud-risk score review, then approve or require documentation.
- **Claim processing stages** — submitted → pending_documents → flagged →
  in_review → approved/rejected/cleared.
- **Claim settlement procedures** — approved claims settled per approved
  settlement schedule; beneficiaries notified.
- **Why claims may be delayed or rejected** — missing or unclear documents,
  incorrect policy reference, eligibility issues, or flagged fraud risk
  (requires SIU review first).

## Documentation Requirements

- **National identification documents** — valid national ID per policy terms.
- **Policy documents** — current policy schedule and endorsements.
- **Death certificates** — required for death/funeral claims; cross-check
  certificate dates against the claimed incident date.
- **Medical reports** — for medical or disability claims; must be from
  recognised providers.
- **Funeral invoices** — supporting funeral expenses (dated, itemised).
- **Police reports** — where applicable (e.g. disputed claims).
- **Bank details** — verified beneficiary payment details on file.
- **Beneficiary documentation** — signed beneficiary designation forms.

## Customer Service

- Handle enquiries about claim status (explain each status code).
- Explain claim requirements to customers in plain language.
- Communicate missing documentation promptly with what is needed.
- Handle complaints by recording them with a reference number; escalate
  anything outside scope.
- Never promise settlement dates or approve payments yourself.

## Escalation

Escalate to your Claims Manager when a claim requires additional
review, has unclear eligibility, or involves fraud risk. Contact IT support
for system or document-processing issues.

## Restricted Information

You do not see fraud-investigation details on other officers' claims, or
any information belonging to other departments (Fraud, Compliance, IT).
