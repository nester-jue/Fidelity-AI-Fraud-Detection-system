# Knowledge Package — Executive Management (CRO)

- Department: executive_management
- Package: executive_management
- Scope: department
- Status: verified
- Owner-role: management

## Overview

Executives need business intelligence, not operational detail. The
assistant gives portfolio performance, exposure trends and strategic views —
with live system numbers where available.

## Company Products and Services

Individual life assurance, funeral assurance, mortgage and loan protection,
endowment policies, group life assurance and annuity products.

## Organisational Structure

Four functional clusters: Claims & Processing, Fraud & Investigations,
Compliance & Governance, Executive Management (this unit); IT and Actuarial
Services sit alongside as cross-function.

## Departmental Responsibilities

- Claims & Processing: registration, assessment, approval and settlement.
- Fraud & Investigations: SIU investigations and fraud exposure.
- Compliance & Governance: regulatory adherence, internal audit, exceptions.
- Executive Management: enterprise risk oversight and strategy execution.

## Business Performance

- Claims received and processed trends (year-on-year and month-on-month).
- Portfolio exposure by policy type and value.
- Fraud detection performance: alerts raised, confirmed fraud rate, AI
  model effectiveness versus historical baselines.
- Claims processing efficiency: average approval turnaround, backlog age.
- Customer service metrics: complaints received, response times,
  volume of POSIT/complaint handling.
- Revenue and financial exposure (outstanding claims value).

## Strategic Objectives

- Improve claims conversion rate; reduce fraudulent payouts; strengthen
  regulatory posture; expand coverage; optimise claims settlement speed.

## Management Reporting Procedures

- Quarterly management review pack: KPI dashboard, trend analysis, risk
  register updates, exception register, remediation plan.
- Board risk reporting as required by policy.
- Executive dashboard refresh on the system for real-time views.

## FAQs

- How many claims were received this month?
- What is the current outstanding claims value?
- Which department has the highest workload?
- How many suspicious claims were identified?
- What is the estimated financial exposure?
- How effective is the fraud detection system?

## Escalation

For macro-level risk, systemic failure or regulatory risk, raise via the
daily executive risk register and escalate to the Board risk committee as
required by company policy.

## Restricted Information

Individual cases without business relevance; no employee personal data
beyond names and roles; external party details are handled per risk policy.
