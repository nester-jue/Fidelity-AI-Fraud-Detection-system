Fidelity Life Assurance — Claims Procedures Manual (SAMPLE — ingest to test Phase 6)

# 1. Scope

This manual applies to Claims Officers, Claims Managers and Fraud
Investigators who process insurance claims under the Claims department policy.
- Department: claims_officer

# 2. Claim Registration

2.1. Claims are registered in the Claims workspace via:
   - The Claims workspace "New Claim" form (Claims Officer, Claims Manager, System Admin).
   - Upload of supporting documents via Document Upload (Claims Officer or Fraud Investigator).

2.2. A registered claim receives a claim reference number (CLM-YYYY-NNNN)
   immediately; the reference is used in all subsequent processing.

2.3. The Claims Officer then:
   - Reviews the AI fraud risk score and indicators.
   - Requests or confirms missing documentation (pending_documents status).
   - Escalates to Fraud Investigators (SIU) if the risk category is High or Critical.

# 3. Supporting Documentation

Required before processing:
   - Valid national identification documents.
   - Policy documents (current schedule, endorsements).
   - Death certificates (for death/funeral claims) — cross-check certificate
     dates against the claimed incident date.
   - Medical reports (medical/disability claims) from recognised providers.
   - Funeral invoices (dated, itemised).
   - Police reports where applicable.
   - Verified beneficiary documentation (signed beneficiary designation).

# 4. Assessment and Approval

4.1. Fraud risk is assessed by the NEXA AI Fraud Detection Engine — a score
   between 0 and 100 with indicators and explanation.

4.2. Claims with risk category Medium/High require secondary review by a
   senior Claims Officer. High and Critical risk claims are escalated to the
   Fraud Investigation Unit; payment is not approved pending SIU review.

4.3. Final approval/rejection is always a human decision; the chatbot and AI
   never approve or reject claims.

# 5. Settlement

Approved claims are settled on the approved settlement schedule; beneficiary
payment details must be verified before disbursement. Rejected claims follow
the Claims department's reconsideration procedure and may be reconsidered
once by the submitting officer, then reviewed again by the manager.
